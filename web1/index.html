About
DOXA

Built on passion and hard work.

DOXA Sports is a new apparel brand from Tirunelveli. We began with a simple dream: make sports wear, casual wear and corporate wear that looks sharp, feels good and arrives on time.

We specialise in bulk manufacturing. Teams, schools, companies and shops can order from ten pieces to many thousands, and we customise names, numbers, logos and colours for each order.

We are just opening our doors, and every customer who trusts us early becomes part of how we grow.
